What happens after you book

We call to confirm

We check your insurance and whether your plan needs a doctor's referral or authorization. If it does, we'll tell you what to do next.

Your evaluation

Your therapist listens, looks at how you move and talks through a plan with you. Bring a photo ID, your insurance card and your new patient forms.

Book your whole plan

Once you know your plan, book every visit at once here, or we'll set them up with you before you leave. Need to change one? Just call.
